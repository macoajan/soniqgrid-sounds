When used in a composition, credit it where other credits are given: "Drum samples provided by DrumGizmo.org".

Changes for this package: the microphone channels of every hit were mixed to one stereo sample with the kit's default mix
(levels, pans, overhead / ambience width; bleed off) and stored as FLAC; ARIA-only opcodes (CC modulation, per-layer
velocity curves, per-note polyphony, choke fade times) were removed; the choke regions play a short silent file.
