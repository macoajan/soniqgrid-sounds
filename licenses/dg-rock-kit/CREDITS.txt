Rock Kit (DrumGizmo)

When used in a composition, credit it where other credits are given: "Drum samples provided by DrumGizmo.org".

Changes for this package: the microphone channels of every hit were mixed to one stereo sample with the kit's default mix
(levels, pans, overhead / ambience width; bleed off) and stored as FLAC; the CC mix modulation was removed (the mix is
fixed); the choke regions play a short silent file. Velocity curves, per-note polyphony and choke fades are kept.
