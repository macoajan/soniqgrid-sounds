Changes for this package: maker and model names were removed from the kit, file and sample names (the kits are called
"Studio Kit" and "Vintage Kit" here), comments were removed from the SFZ files, and the samples were converted losslessly
from WAV to FLAC; a mistyped sample path in the 4-piece kit was fixed. The recordings, mapping, levels and pans are unchanged. This modified sample set is shared under the
same licence. Music made with these samples may be licensed any way you like.
